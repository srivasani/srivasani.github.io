# Micro Waves

- Frequency: 3 GHz- 100 GHz
- Unidirectional
- LOS is mandatory
- Applications
  1. Backhaul communication between mobile towers
  2. Satellite TV broadcasting


### Types

#### 1.	Terrestrial Microwave
- Communication between towers (30 to 50 km)

#### 2.	Satellite Microwave
- Earth to satellite and satellite to earth communication
- Transmission Frequence: Ku Band (12 to 18 GHz).
