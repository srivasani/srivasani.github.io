# Radio Waves

- Omnidirectional

### 1. High Frequency

#### Frequency Range
- 3 to 30 MHz

#### Wave length
- 10 to 100 meter

#### Propagation Method
- Ionospherin (sky propagation)

#### Distance
- Thousands of km

#### Applications

1. Internation Radio (AM)
2. Disaster Management



---

### 2. Very High Frequency

#### Frequency Range
- 30 to 300 MHz

#### Wave length
- 1 to 10 m

#### Propagation Method
- Line of Sight + Ground Wave
(ground propogation)


#### Distance

- About 100 km

#### Applications

1. FM Radio Broadcasting
2. ATC (Air Traffic Control)


---

### 3. Ultra High Frequency

#### Frequency Range
- 300 MHz to 3 GHz

#### Wave length
- 1 m to 10 cm

#### Propagation Method
- Line of sight

#### Distance
- About 50 km

#### Applications

1. Mobile communication
2. Digital television
3. Bluetooth
4. Wi-Fi
5. Radar
6. GPS
7. Walkie-Talkie
